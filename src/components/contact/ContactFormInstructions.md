
# Contact Form Functionality

The contact form offers several user-friendly options for submitting inquiries:

## How It Works

1. **Form Submission**: When visitors complete the contact form and click "Send Message":
   - Their message details are captured and formatted
   - A confirmation message is displayed
   - The submission is logged in the console (for development purposes)

2. **After submission**, visitors have additional options:
   - **Open in Email App**: Opens the default email client with the form information pre-filled
   - **Download as Text**: Generates a formatted text file with the message details
   - **Send Another Message**: Clears the form for a new submission

## Important Notes:

- No external services or accounts are required
- Form validation ensures all required fields are completed before submission
- The form provides visual feedback during submission with a loading indicator
- After submission, users receive confirmation that their message was received
- For development and testing, all submissions are logged to the console

## Troubleshooting:

If you encounter any issues with the contact form:

1. Ensure all required fields are completed
2. Check that a valid email format is entered
3. For direct contact, use the phone number or email address displayed in the contact cards
