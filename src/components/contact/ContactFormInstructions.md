
# Contact Form Functionality

The contact form offers several user-friendly options for submitting inquiries:

## How It Works

1. **Form Submission**: When visitors complete the contact form and click "Send Message":
   - Their message details are captured and stored temporarily
   - A confirmation message is displayed explaining the next steps
   - Users are provided with options to complete their message submission

2. **After submission**, visitors have these options:
   - **Open in Email App**: Opens the default email client with the form information pre-filled for immediate sending
   - **Download as Text**: Generates a formatted text file with the message details to save locally
   - **Send Another Message**: Clears the form for a new submission

## Important Notes:

- This form does not automatically send emails to our team
- To complete your message submission, please use one of the post-submission options (Email App or Download)
- For immediate assistance, please use the direct phone number or email address displayed in the contact cards
- Form validation ensures all required fields are completed before submission

## Troubleshooting:

If you encounter any issues with the contact form:

1. Ensure all required fields are completed
2. Check that a valid email format is entered
3. For direct contact, use the phone number or email address displayed in the contact cards
